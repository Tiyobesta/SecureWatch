Cybersecurity Guidelines

Threats and Preventive Measures

Threat Preventive Measure
Phishing Do not click suspicious links.
Malware Use updated antivirus software.
Weak Passwords Use strong and unique passwords.

1. Password Security

  • Use at least 12 characters.
  • Mix letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Never reuse passwords across sites.

2. Email and Phishing Protection

  • Verify the sender's email address.
  • Hover over links before clicking.
  • Look out for urgent or threatening language.

3. Safe Internet Practices

  • Keep your operating system updated.
  • Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

4. Incident Response Steps

  1. Disconnect the affected device from the network.
  2. Report the incident immediately using the Report page.
  3. Keep the device available for the security team to investigate.
  4. Change your passwords from a different, safe device.
